An update to Turok 3: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: widescreen drawn out to the window's edges, multiplayer for up to four players, and less work a frame.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- The renderer finds the picture where the game's own video mode points, so the game's video mode is left as it set it, no longer moved a row to make it findable.
- In widescreen the picture reaches the window's right edge (a black strip down it is gone), the opening scene's black bars cover the whole width at 21:9 with no black lines down the picture between them, and the black strip along the bottom edge is thinner.
- In a window wider than 16:9, the swirl behind the boot screens, the title and the menus reaches the window's edges: its corners and sides are no longer black.
- In a window up to 21:9 wide the level's rooms at the picture's sides are drawn: the view the game picks the rooms to draw by is widened too, not only the one it culls models against.
- In widescreen the on-screen messages stay whole in the middle of the window: the Breach's "New Objective" no longer comes apart, its two halves at the window's sides (or with a gap between them at 4:3).
- In widescreen the Breach's first cutscene (the tentacles at the window) has no black lines down its picture: its letterbox's side border is taken out, as the opening scene's is.
- Multiplayer offers up to four players: the keyboard and the first controller play as player 1 and each further controller as the next player, in the order they were connected.
- The renderer no longer copies each frame back into the console's memory, which the game never reads, so each frame takes less work; Copy Frames to RAM on the Graphics tab, Off by default, turns the copy back on if something ever looks wrong without it.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok3Recompiled-v0.2.2-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok3Recompiled-v0.2.2-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `a2e0a28dc0d3a48c7c02cb9e4c4b38fe04b2d436`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the Breach, Joseph's path, Deathmatch, saving to and loading from the Controller Pak, long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-3-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.
