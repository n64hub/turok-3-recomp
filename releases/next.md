- **Less input lag:** each frame waited most of its time with the pad it read already old. The game now starts each frame just in time for its turn on the screen and shows it as soon as it is drawn. Measured from a press of the stick or a key to the picture changing, the median is about 80 ms instead of 135 ms (of which about 70 ms is the measurement's own capture of the window). Where the game runs slower than 30 frames a second (the cutscenes), frames start and show as before.
- **MS Anti-Aliasing** goes up to **8X**, where the GPU can take it (greyed out where it can't). 2X stays the default.
