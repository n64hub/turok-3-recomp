An update to Turok 3: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: with a gamepad, looking up and down with the stick moves the view at once and the view stays where you leave it.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Fixed:**

- With a gamepad, looking up and down with the right stick (and the arrow keys) now moves the view at once, at a speed set by how far the stick is pushed, and the view stays where you leave it. Before, the view eased slowly towards the stick's angle and sprang back level as soon as the stick was let go, so an aim up or down couldn't be held.

**Windows:** unzip `Turok3Recompiled-v0.2.7-windows-x64.zip` and run `Turok3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `Turok3Recompiled-v0.2.7-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `Turok3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `a2e0a28dc0d3a48c7c02cb9e4c4b38fe04b2d436`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the Breach, Joseph's path, a whole Deathmatch, saving to and loading from the Controller Pak, long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-3-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.
