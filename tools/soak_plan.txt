# Turok 3's soak plan: one round through the game's modes and scenes for the desktop soak, a play of its Linux build by
# this plan for 180 s that fails on a crash, the program exiting or the picture standing still for 10 s. Each line is a
# step at seconds since the program started: key <name> [ms held], down and up <name>, shot <name>, until <addr> <mask>
# <value> <timeout s> [key [ms [every s]]] (waits until the game's 32-bit word at addr, anded with mask, is value,
# pressing key, held ms (120), every s (1.5) meanwhile; the later steps move on by the wait, and a wait unmet fails the
# play), and random <key>,... (one of them every 0.3-1.2 s) until random off. The keys are src/game/config.cpp's (Enter
# Start, E A, Q B, W A S D the C buttons, the arrows the stick, Space R).
#
# A round: the boot, Start through the logos to the title and its menu, Deathmatch (its mode, players, the Rumble Pak
# notice) into a Bloodlust match against a bot: 10 s of random moves, turns, jumps and weapon changes, the pause menu
# (Start), Quit and Yes to its results and the match's setup, B (Q) back to the title menu, whose cursor is back on New
# Game, then New Game, Normal, the Rumble Pak notice, the opening scene skipped to Choose, Danielle and her scene into
# the first level, the Breach: 10 s of random input, the pause menu with its Options opened and left by B, Resume, and
# random input to the end. The arena and the Breach are the round's level change. There is no decomp: the words were
# read as the game played. The word at 0x801029D4 is the screen the game is running (the code it runs for it): 0x418A68
# the title, 0x419178 its menu, 0x419570 Deathmatch's mode, 0x41959C its players, 0x4191D8 its Rumble Pak notice,
# 0x418D18 a match, 0x419860 its pause menu, 0x419834 its results, 0x419808 its setup, 0x4191AC the difficulty,
# 0x419204 New Game's Rumble Pak notice, 0x21ECB0 the opening, 0x419384 Choose, 0x21ECDC Danielle's scene. Each `until`
# on it presses on, every 4 s and held 400 ms, until the next screen is up, so a press the game drops (the notices
# drop some) is pressed again and a slow load is waited for. The word at 0x8011D5A4 is 0 in a level, and the one at
# 0x8011D510 is 7 while the Breach's pause menu is open. The word at 0x80102E84 (tools/survey.js) is 1 once the boot is
# past its first stage; past that it climbs through each screen's own states, so it tells no screen from another.
